# Privacy Policy - I Know You

Last updated: 2026-10-06

I Know You does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no ads,
no analytics, and requests no permissions.

Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its
data deletes them. If you use the Share button, your device's share sheet opens and only what you
choose to share leaves the app, sent by the app you pick.
